# NAFIA Surgical Mart — design direction

## Design movement
An editorial medtech storefront: confident, calm, tactile and quietly premium rather than generic hospital blue, discount commerce or a heavy corporate template. Keep the existing company identity—deep green, warm ivory and gold—and make it feel considered through composition, whitespace, fine rules and purposeful type.

## Core principles
1. Make the next useful action obvious: call, message, check product size or confirm a delivery area.
2. Write for real procurement circumstances: clear quantities, packaging, sizes, cautions and what to ask before traveling or placing an order.
3. Earn trust through precise language. Mark package-printed information as such; do not imply independent certification, clinical safety beyond package details, local stock, delivery reach or guarantees.
4. Maintain speed and readability on common mobile devices and constrained connections. Use a small number of purposeful visual assets, system-first type and short, GPU-friendly motion.
5. Make all content and controls work with keyboard, assistive technology and reduced-motion preferences.

## Color philosophy
- Deep evergreen `#123b34` anchors the brand, navigation and dark sections.
- Warm ivory `#f7f5ef` is the generous reading canvas; white supports clean product surfaces.
- Restrained antique gold `#c6a45c` marks the brand, focus accents and primary action without sacrificing contrast.
- Ink `#172320`, muted slate and a pale sage tint support legibility and section hierarchy.
- Keep colors solid and functional; use a low-contrast tonal wash only for atmospheric hero depth.

## Layout paradigm
Responsive editorial split-hero with a clear text column and one product-art focal area; full-width trust/packaging strip; readable alternating editorial sections; structured product details, order-help cards, quality facts, buying steps, FAQs and contact. Use a consistent content grid and spacing scale, broad section margins on desktop and a compact single-column flow on mobile. Navigation remains accessible and mobile actions are thumb-friendly.

## Signature elements
A distinctive NAFIA icon built from a gloved-hand/medical plus motif, small gold rules and quiet index-like section labels. The hero uses a soft studio-style glove illustration/image framed by thin orbital paths and small factual callouts, not intrusive parallax. A focused product information card prioritizes size, packaging and allergy/single-use cautions. The delivery-area helper makes it easy to ask whether a particular district/upazila can be served without suggesting a promise.

## Interaction philosophy
Interactions are direct, predictable, keyboard-visible and mobile-friendly. Size selection updates the WhatsApp quotation message; quotation inputs are labeled and preserved in an encoded draft link. A compact mobile menu closes on navigation and Escape. FAQs expose their expanded state. Avoid form submissions that imply data was sent before WhatsApp opens.

## Animation
Animate only the hero's single visual group with a slow, small translate/rotate and restrained orbital stroke/float. Prefer CSS transform and opacity, keep looping motions subtle, disable them in `prefers-reduced-motion`, and avoid scroll hijacking, continuous blur/filter work, heavy canvas and animation libraries. The page should read beautifully with all motion disabled.

## Typography system
Use a refined system-first sans stack for body and controls; pair a familiar Georgia/serif editorial headline with disciplined weight and line height. Keep body copy comfortably sized, headings short, labels uppercase only in small metadata, and avoid thin low-contrast text. Do not block rendering on remote fonts.

## Brand essence and voice
NAFIA is a practical local supplier serving care teams and resellers from Chattogram. The voice is respectful, clear, helpful and factual—simple enough to scan, specific enough to order confidently, and never grandiose or medically prescriptive.

## Wordmark/logo
Create a simple recognizable NAFIA mark: an abstract folded surgical glove/hand and medical cross represented with broad, flat shapes and negative space. Use evergreen and gold over warm ivory for web presentation, with a separate opaque full-bleed square icon for the project/favicon. No tiny text, bevels, gradients or complex effects.

## Signature brand color
Deep evergreen `#123b34`.

## Hero art brief
One original, wide hero visual for the supplier website: a single pale warm-ivory natural-rubber surgical glove in an elegant upright three-quarter/palm-facing pose, isolated against a softly lit warm-ivory studio field with ample calm negative space and subtle evergreen/gold accents. Premium editorial product photography/3D-render sensibility, credible soft material, natural proportions, gentle directional shadows. No package, logos, text, people, blood, instruments or medical claims. This art is illustrative, not a verified representation of a particular NAFIA package. The page will frame the image and apply only a subtle GPU-friendly slow float with CSS.
